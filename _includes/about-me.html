Hi, I'm Alessandro, a game developer who's spent the last seven years building interactive experiences, mostly in Unity and Unreal Engine. I started my career in London testing games and making sure Italian translations didn't sound ridiculous, which taught me to care deeply about the little details that make games feel polished.

These days, I split my time between professional work optimizing systems, building features for live games, and keeping tens of thousands of players engaged and personal projects where I get to experiment freely. I'm fascinated by the intersection of code and art: writing custom shaders, making complex 3D models, finding ways to bring digital creations to life. There's something satisfying about making digital things behave like their physical counterparts.

I'm naturally curious, which means I'm always learning something new. Lately, I've been exploring how AI can accelerate development without losing the craft, diving into procedural animation systems, and revisiting childhood memories through 3D, like recreating that vintage lava lamp that's been sitting on my shelf for decades, or modeling my dad's custom-built RC car. I also have a soft spot for modding classic games and pushing old engines to do things they weren't quite designed for.
