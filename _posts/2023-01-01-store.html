The store is where players can pick up in-game items and resources, either with real money or in-game currency. There’s a bit of everything: special offers, exclusive items, and currency packs.

This is the latest version of the store, completely redesigned and rebuilt from scratch. The goal was to give it a fresh look and boost monetization. Since most of the logic is on the client side (with just data configuration on the server), I handled the whole build myself, focusing on a few key points:

  • Single Scroll: The store scrolls vertically in portrait and horizontally in landscape. Each section’s layout adapts smoothly to the screen orientation.
  • Section Types: The store is split into different sections, each with its own style and purpose. I built a modular system so it’s easy to add, remove, or tweak sections as needed.
  • Section Ordering: The order of sections is set by a server config, which makes A/B testing and experimenting with layouts simple.
  • Section Refreshing: Each section can update its content on its own, like when an offer expires or a condition changes. The whole store can also update and rearrange itself without a full app reload.
  • Section Customization: Sections like Special Offers are customizable through AssetBundles or Addressables, so new content and updates can go live quickly, without needing a full app release.

Portrait orientation

The art team provided all the graphic assets, but I handled everything else: animations, effects, and implementation. I used Unity’s UI system (uGUI), DOTween, the Animator, and some custom shaders and particle systems for the visuals.

Burraco works in both portrait and landscape, so the store had to feel right in either mode. Each layout adapts on the fly; no need to duplicate elements, since everything just rearranges itself depending on the orientation.
